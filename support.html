Frequently asked questions

Where is my weight data stored?

Your weight history, targets, progress phases, and app settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Weight Tracker does not require an account or send your weight data to the developer.

How do I back up or move my entries?

In History, choose Export Weight History to save your weigh-ins as a CSV file. The CSV contains dates and weights only; targets, progress phases, and app settings are not included. Import Weight History can add compatible weigh-ins later. Dates already in the app and duplicate dates in the file are skipped.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual weigh-ins from History. To remove the app’s locally stored data, delete Weight Tracker rather than merely removing it from the Home Screen or offloading it. Exported CSV files and device backups must be managed separately.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

In Weight Tracker, open Weigh-In Reminder and confirm that it is enabled. Then open iPhone Settings and verify that notifications are allowed for Weight Tracker.

Do optional tips unlock features?

No. Tips are optional consumable purchases that support development. They can be purchased more than once and do not change app functionality.
