Frequently asked questions

Where is my weight data stored?

Your entries and target are stored locally on your iPhone. Weight Tracker does not require an account or upload your weight history to the developer.

How do I back up or move my entries?

Open History and use Export CSV to save a copy. Use Import CSV to bring compatible entries back into Weight Tracker.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Open the app’s reminder settings and confirm the reminder is enabled. Also check iPhone Settings > Notifications > Weight Tracker to make sure notifications are allowed.

Do optional tips unlock features?

No. Tips are optional consumable purchases that support development. They can be purchased more than once and do not change app functionality.
