Privacy Policy

Weight Tracker is designed to work without an account and without sending your weight history to the developer.

Information stored by the app

Weight Tracker stores the information you enter—including weight entries, an optional target weight, unit preferences, reminder settings, and related app state—locally on your device using Apple’s storage frameworks.

Data collection and tracking

Weight Tracker does not send your weight entries, targets, or reminder settings to the developer. The app does not use developer-operated servers, user accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell personal information or share it for advertising or tracking.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you include. Support messages may be used to respond, troubleshoot problems, and improve the app. They are handled by the email services used by you and the developer.

Exports and device backups

CSV exports contain weigh-in dates and weights; targets, progress phases, and app settings are not included. You choose where an exported file is saved or shared. After export, the file is controlled by you and by any service you choose to use. Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, app data may also be included in backups managed by Apple. Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable a daily weigh-in reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules the reminder on your device. Weight Tracker does not use notifications for advertising or transmit reminder settings to the developer.

Optional purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Weight Tracker receives the purchase status and receipt information needed to complete the transaction, but the developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles purchase and account information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual weigh-ins within the app. Deleting Weight Tracker—not merely removing it from the Home Screen or offloading it—removes its locally stored app data from that iPhone. Exported CSV files remain wherever you saved or shared them, and copies may remain in device backups depending on your backup settings. Because Weight Tracker has no account or developer-operated server, there is no server-side profile to delete.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page with its latest revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to weighttrackerapp.support@gmail.com.
