Privacy Policy

Weight Tracker is designed to work without an account and without sending your weight history to the developer.

Information stored by the app

Weight Tracker stores the information you enter—including weight entries, an optional target weight, unit preferences, reminder settings, and related app state—locally on your device using Apple’s storage frameworks.

Data collection and tracking

Weight Tracker does not transmit your weight entries or target to the developer. The app does not use developer-operated servers, user accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell or share your personal data.

Exports and device backups

If you export a CSV file, you choose where that file is saved or shared. After export, the file is controlled by you and by any service you choose to use. Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, app data may also be included in backups managed by Apple. Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable a daily weigh-in reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules the reminder on your device. Weight Tracker does not use notifications for advertising or transmit reminder settings to the developer.

Optional purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries within the app. Removing Weight Tracker from your device removes its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups you control. Because the developer does not operate an account or server for the app, there is no server-side profile to delete.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page with its latest revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to weighttrackerapp.support@gmail.com.
